3. **Connect to OKX NFT**: Open the NFT marketplace at the Web3 portal and connect your wallet.

4. **Browse or search**: Explore the marketplace by trending collections, category, blockchain, or search for a specific collection by name.

5. **Review before buying**: Check the NFT's properties — rarity score, trading history, active offers, verified collection description, and floor price. OKX displays all this data on the detail page.

8. **View your NFT**: Your purchased NFT appears in your wallet portfolio under the NFT section.

> **Tip**: If you're buying from a collection with a whitelist mechanism, you may encounter a "third-party contract execution failed" error. This means your wallet address isn't on the project's whitelist. Contact the project team to confirm eligibility.
